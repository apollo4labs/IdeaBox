# Decentralized & Privacy Ideas

Collection of ideas focused on decentralization, self-sovereign infrastructure, and privacy-respecting systems. These are the projects where the architecture is the product — where the design choices themselves are the value proposition.

## Current Ideas

### Anonymous Decentralized Chat
A censorship-resistant messenger where every user runs their own node, messages route through Tor, and identity is a cryptographic onion address — no accounts, no central server, no public IP required. Built in Rust with embedded Tor (arti), Ed25519 identity, and offline message queuing.

**Status:** Concept
**Folder:** `topics/decentralized/anonymous-chat/`

## Why This Category Exists

Centralized services can be compelled. Federated services can be pressured. Even end-to-end encryption is at risk when client-side scanning becomes legal mandate. Decentralized, self-hosted systems offer a structural answer: there is no operator to compel, no plaintext to scan, no central point to take down.

The ideas in this folder are not "products" in the traditional sense. They are infrastructure for a world where privacy is the default, not a premium feature.
